About TakeHomeSalaryCalc.com

Most salary calculators tell you your tax bracket and stop. That's not what people actually need when they're staring at a paycheck that feels wrong.

This tool was built for a specific moment: you just got paid, the number is lower than you expected, and you need to understand why. Not in theory — in your actual numbers, with every deduction traced line by line.

We show gross salary, every pre-tax deduction, taxable income, each piece of FICA separately, post-tax deductions, and the real take-home number broken down by year, month, and paycheck. No black box. No hidden assumptions.

The calculations use 2025 federal tax brackets, current standard deductions, and accurate FICA rates including the Additional Medicare tax above $200K. State taxes and credits are not included — this is federal-only, designed to give you a clear, traceable baseline.

TakeHomeSalaryCalc.com is part of the Calc-HQ network of financial calculators.
