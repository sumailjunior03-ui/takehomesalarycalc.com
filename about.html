About TakeHomeSalaryCalc.com

Purpose of This Calculator

TakeHomeSalaryCalc.com provides a browser-based estimate of how much gross salary may remain after U.S. federal income tax and optional FICA. It is designed for salary planning, budgeting, and compensation comparison.

Who This Tool Is For

This tool is useful for employees reviewing job offers, comparing raises, and anyone who wants a quick estimate of monthly or annual take-home pay using a federal-only model.

How The Calculator Works

The calculator applies the standard deduction tied to the selected filing status, estimates federal income tax using progressive tax brackets, and optionally includes Social Security and Medicare taxes. It then converts the estimated net annual figure into monthly, bi-weekly, and weekly amounts.

Data Sources and Scope

This calculator uses a deterministic federal-only methodology. It does not include state taxes, retirement deferrals, insurance deductions, tax credits, or employer-specific payroll withholding adjustments.
